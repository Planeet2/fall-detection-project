# Daily Care Rules / 生活关怀规则

## Medicine Reminder

Morning reminder: 08:00  
Evening reminder: 20:00  

Example message:
"Please remember to take your blood pressure medicine."

## Weather Reminder

Before going outside, remind the elder to check the weather.

Example message:
"It may be cold outside today. Please wear warm clothes."

## Health Suggestion

If abnormal walking or possible fall risk is detected:
- suggest resting;
- suggest measuring blood pressure;
- notify family if the risk is high.
